## PART 2 — CLASSIFY BY ROOT CAUSE (20 min)

Errors are not random. They cluster, and a cluster is a **habit**, not a
collection of bad luck. Sort your log into these four buckets and count.

| cause tag | what it looks like | my count |
|---|---|---|
| **sign** | `−` became `+` when moving a term; wrong root sign | |
| **distribution** | forgot the multiply over a sum; `(x+2)² ≠ x²+2` | |
| **formula** | wrote the quadratic formula wrong; misremembered the modular rule | |
| **careless** | right method, wrong digit, one transcription slip | |

Now the two questions that matter:

- ☐  My largest bucket is ______ with ______ entries.
- ☐  Is my largest bucket **careless**? Yes / No

**If the answer is yes, that is genuinely good news**, and I want to explain why,
because students always read it the other way round. A pile of careless errors
means your method is sound and your transcription is not — and transcription is
fixable in a week by writing more slowly. A pile of *formula* errors means you
have the mathematics wrong, which takes longer and is the thing you actually
need to know about yourself.

A pile of **sign** errors is the most common outcome in this unit, and it is the
most fixable: signs are lost almost exclusively at the moment of *moving a term
across an equals sign*, and the fix is a rule you write down once.

- ☐  Write your personal rule for moving a term across `=` without losing its
     sign. In your own words, one sentence: ______
- ☐  If your largest bucket is distribution, write the example you got wrong and
     the correct expansion beside it: ______

## 🇹🇼 TAIWAN CONTEXT

The distinction in Part 2 between *careless* and *formula* errors is the same
one that separates a typo from a misunderstanding in any subject, and it is
worth carrying past this course. In a language you are learning, a wrong
conjugation is usually a rule you do not know; a wrong spelling of a word you
know is usually carelessness. Same split, same fix, same amount of time. Three
days of study left — spend them on whichever bucket you actually have.
