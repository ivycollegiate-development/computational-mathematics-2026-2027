## Course Description
This full-year course (prerequisite: Pre-Calculus) explores mathematics through programming. You'll use Python to solve problems in algebra, statistics, probability, geometry, and calculus — while building an awareness of how math powers privacy and security in the real world. Project-based, one multi-week project per unit.

**Textbook:** *Doing Math with Python* by Amit Saha (No Starch Press, 2015)

## Weekly Rhythm
