# U0 L01 — Day 1: Paper Survey & Welcome (PAPER)

**Unit:** 0 — Course Survey
**Type:** Paper (survey + discussion, 45 minutes)
**LO:** gauge math comfort, programming exposure, and security awareness; collect baseline data for Unit 1 pacing

---

## TODAY'S PURPOSE

First real class day. You get a paper survey — no computer, no pressure, no wrong answers. I read every response before Day 2 so I can pace Unit 1 to this specific class.

---

## PART 1 — WELCOME (5 min)

- Who I am, what this class is
- **Computational Mathematics** = where computers do the arithmetic and humans do the strategy
- The year in 30 seconds: 7 units, 7 projects, you build things that work

---

## PART 2 — SURVEY (25 min)

**Materials:** Printed survey (one per student)

Work independently. Be honest — this is not graded. "Never coded before" is exactly as valuable as "I build projects independently" — it tells me how to teach you.

**Survey sections:**
1. **Math background** — what you've taken, how confident you feel on each topic
2. **Programming experience** — languages, tools, self-assessment
3. **Technology & security awareness** — what you use, what you've experienced
4. **Goals & interests** — what you want from this class, what you're curious about

---

## PART 3 — COLLECT & QUICK DISCUSS (15 min)

Surveys in. While I sort them, volunteers share **one** interesting answer — any section, any question. No judgment, just "that's a real perspective."

---

## TURN IN

**Survey paper** — handed to me before you leave. Name on it.

---

## 📋 TOMORROW

**Next:** U0 L02 — What Is Computational Mathematics? (Discussion/overview). No homework. No prep. Just show up.

## 🇹🇼 TAIWAN CONTEXT

The first-day survey is how every ICA course starts — not to sort you into "good at math" and "bad at math," but to design the next 170 days for the humans actually in the room. The data from this survey shapes whether Unit 1 starts with REPL exploration or jumps straight to function definitions.
